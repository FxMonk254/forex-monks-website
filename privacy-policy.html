Plain-language summary

Your privacy matters.

We collect only the information needed to operate accounts, deliver app features, manage access, send notifications, support advertising, maintain security and improve reliability. We do not sell personal information.

01

Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Forex Monks Company Android application, this website and related support services, collectively referred to as the “Services.”

Forex Monks Company provides market information, app notifications, account-based content and related features. The Services do not execute trades, hold client funds or operate as a brokerage.

02

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Name and email address.
  • Login credentials handled through Firebase Authentication.
  • Information included in messages sent to customer support.

Account and access information

  • Firebase user identifier and email-verification status.
  • Account role, VIP status and subscription-plan designation.
  • Account creation, update and last-login timestamps.
  • Notification preferences.

Device and technical information

  • Expo push-notification token and device platform.
  • Basic device, app-version and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and related technical information that may be processed by Google AdMob.

We do not collect payment-card or bank-account details inside the app. Any external payment service used for a membership is governed by that provider’s own terms and privacy practices.

03

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create, verify, secure and manage user accounts.
  • Provide content and features available to each account.
  • Manage free, VIP and administrative access permissions.
  • Deliver requested push notifications.
  • Display and measure advertising for eligible users.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Detect misuse, troubleshoot problems and improve reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

05

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob. Eligible free users may be shown banner or rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad impressions, interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, secure and measure advertisements.

The availability and personalization of advertisements may depend on your device settings, region, consent choices and Google’s policies. VIP and administrative accounts may be configured to bypass advertisements.

06

Push notifications

With your permission, we store an Expo push token associated with your account so we can send app, content and service updates. Notification permission is optional.

You may disable notifications using the app’s preference controls where available or through your Android device settings. Disabling notifications does not prevent you from using the rest of the app.

07

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited information:

  • With the service providers described in this policy.
  • When required by law or a valid legal request.
  • To protect users, our rights, security or the Services.
  • In connection with a legitimate business restructuring, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.
08

Data retention

We retain account information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

When an account is deleted, the active Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore user profile are scheduled for deletion through our deletion process. Limited information may remain temporarily in secure backups or be retained where required for fraud prevention, security or legal compliance.

09

Account and data deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly inside the app:

  1. Open the Profile screen.
  2. Select Privacy & Security.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm the deletion.

If you cannot access the app, you may initiate an external deletion request by emailing us from the address associated with your account. We may request reasonable verification before deleting the account to protect it from unauthorized requests.

Request account deletion

Account deletion is permanent. Information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes will be handled as explained in the retention section above.

10

Your privacy choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account. You may also update your profile, manage notification permission, adjust device advertising settings or contact us with a privacy question.

To protect account information, we may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

11

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information, including authenticated access and security controls provided through Firebase. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting your password and device. Please contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

12

Age requirement

The Services are intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that an underage person has provided personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

A parent or guardian who believes that a minor has created an account may contact us using the details below.

13

International data processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Privacy and data-protection laws may differ between jurisdictions. Providers apply their own contractual and technical safeguards as described in their policies.

14
